1. The Einstein Field Equations

The EFE links mass-energy to spacetime curvature. The right-hand side describes matter content; the left-hand side describes geometry.

Gμν = κTμν

Compact form

Gμν is the Einstein tensor, and Tμν is the mass-energy tensor. The constant κ = 8πG/c4 converts physical units into geometric curvature.

Gμν + Λgμν = κTμν

Cosmological form

The Λgμν term behaves like a uniform background contribution from spacetime itself. A positive Λ is associated with the accelerated expansion of the universe.

The reading is compact: matter tells spacetime how to curve. Curvature tells matter how to move. Sources shape geometry, and geometry determines natural motion.

2. Riemannian Geometry in Curved Spacetime

The EFE describes curved spacetime, and the language of curved spacetime is Riemannian geometry. More precisely, relativity uses pseudo-Riemannian geometry because of the spacetime metric signature.

Manifold

Spacetime as a 4D manifold

Spacetime is locally flat: in a small enough region it looks like ℝ4. Globally, however, it can be curved. At each point there is a tangent space where physical quantities such as velocity and momentum live.

gμν

Metric tensor

The metric tensor encodes the geometry of spacetime. It defines distances, angles, volumes, proper time, and causal structure.

Γρμν

Christoffel symbols

Christoffel symbols define the connection. They tell us how to differentiate and parallel transport vectors consistently across a curved manifold, and they are computed from the metric and its derivatives.

Rρσμν

Riemann curvature tensor

The Riemann tensor measures intrinsic curvature. If a vector is transported around a closed loop and returns rotated, that mismatch encodes curvature.

3. Building the Einstein Tensor

The slide sequence starts with the metric, compresses curvature information step by step, and ends with the divergence-free curvature object that can match conservation.

1

Metric tensor

gμν defines distance and angle at every point. It is the starting point of the geometry.

2

Christoffel symbols

They describe how basis vectors change as we move across the manifold, making covariant derivatives possible.

3

Riemann curvature tensor

This tensor contains the full intrinsic curvature of spacetime.

4

Ricci tensor

Rμν = Rρμρν. This contraction keeps the information about how volumes change under curvature.

5

Ricci scalar

R = gμνRμν. This is one scalar number describing total curvature at a point.

6

Einstein tensor

Gμν = Rμν - 1/2 Rgμν. This is the divergence-free combination of Rμν and R.

The useful order is: metric → connection → Riemann curvature → Ricci tensor → Ricci scalar → Einstein tensor. The EFE connects this final curvature package to stress-energy.

4. Energy-Momentum Tensor

Tμν is the source of the gravitational field in Einstein's gravity. It contains not only mass, but also energy, momentum flow, pressure, and shear.

Component Physical interpretation Plain reading
T00 Energy density, time-time component How much energy exists per unit volume.
T0i Energy flux, time-space component How much energy flows through a unit area.
Ti0 Momentum density, space-time component How much momentum exists per unit volume.
Tij Stress, space-space component How pressure and shear forces act per unit area.
Physics requires energy-momentum conservation. Therefore the source side must satisfy ∇μTμν = 0, which is why the Bianchi identity matters.

5. Bianchi Identity

Instead of reproducing the full contraction proof, this section summarizes what the Bianchi identity is and why it is important for the EFE.

What

A geometric constraint on curvature

The Bianchi identity says curvature cannot vary arbitrarily. In the slide language, the covariant exterior derivative of curvature vanishes identically.

Contracted

The Einstein tensor has zero divergence

After contraction, the identity gives ∇μGμν = 0. This is why the Einstein tensor is the natural divergence-free curvature tensor.

Why

The EFE must match conservation

If Gμν is to equal Tμν up to constants, the geometry side must have the same conservation structure as the matter side: ∇μTμν = 0.

6. Solving the EFE

The EFE is a nonlinear PDE system with 10 independent equations from the symmetric 4×4 rank-2 tensor. A general analytic solution is essentially impossible.

Symmetry

Analytic solutions

Strong symmetry assumptions and the vacuum condition Tμν = 0 can simplify the equations dramatically. Minkowski, Schwarzschild, and Kerr metrics are representative examples.

Numerical

Numerical relativity

Real systems such as binary black holes and neutron-star mergers usually have no ideal symmetry. Numerical relativity integrates the EFE computationally to produce gravitational-wave waveforms, ray tracing, and fields near black holes.

Metric Assumption Description
Minkowski Flat spacetime, no matter The stage of special relativity.
Schwarzschild Spherical, non-rotating, vacuum A static black-hole geometry.
Kerr Axisymmetric, rotating, vacuum A rotating black-hole geometry.

7. Current Game Calculation

The current game is not full numerical relativity. The curved spacetime on screen is drawn as a potential-like height field, while Ally's trajectory is integrated using softened Newtonian acceleration.

h(x, y)

Curved spacetime visual

For each mass well i, the game computes a softened distance and turns it into a negative potential-like surface height. Larger or closer masses pull the grid farther downward.

ri = √((x - xi)2 + (y - yi)2 + ε2)
h(x, y) = -zScale · Σi mi / ri 
function heightAt(x, y) {
  let sum = 0;
  for (const mass of state.level.masses) {
    const r = Math.hypot(x - mass.x, y - mass.y, softening);
    sum += mass.mass / r;
  }
  return -zScale * sum;
}
softening = 0.35 zScale = 0.98 extent = 7.5

The warped grid is not a live metric solver. It is a height proxy generated from the mass layout, but it still visualizes the intuition that sources change geometry.

Newtonian trajectory

Ally's 2D trajectory is computed by summing an inverse-square-like force from each mass well. Softening prevents the acceleration from becoming infinite at the center of a well.

r⃗i = (x - xi, y - yi)
a⃗ = Σi -gParticle · mi · r⃗i / (|r⃗i|2 + ε2)3/2 
function particleAccel(x, y) {
  let ax = 0;
  let ay = 0;
  for (const mass of state.level.masses) {
    const rx = x - mass.x;
    const ry = y - mass.y;
    const rsq = rx * rx + ry * ry + softening * softening;
    const amp = (-gParticle * mass.mass) / (rsq ** 1.5);
    ax += amp * rx;
    ay += amp * ry;
  }
  const n = Math.hypot(ax, ay);
  if (n > maxAccel) {
    const s = maxAccel / n;
    ax *= s;
    ay *= s;
  }
  return { ax, ay };
}

1. The Einstein Field Equations

EFE는 mass-energy와 spacetime curvature를 한 식에 묶습니다. 오른쪽은 matter content, 왼쪽은 geometry입니다.

Gμν = κTμν

Compact form

Gμν는 Einstein tensor, Tμν는 mass-energy tensor입니다. κ = 8πG/c4는 물리량을 기하학적 곡률로 바꾸는 coupling constant입니다.

Gμν + Λgμν = κTμν

Cosmological form

Λgμν는 spacetime 자체의 균일한 배경 항처럼 작동합니다. Λ > 0이면 우주의 accelerating expansion과 연결됩니다.

읽는 방향은 간단합니다. Matter tells spacetime how to curve. Curvature tells matter how to move. 즉, source가 geometry를 만들고 geometry가 motion을 정합니다.

2. Riemannian Geometry in Curved Spacetime

EFE가 설명하는 것은 curved spacetime이고, curved spacetime을 말하는 언어가 Riemannian geometry입니다. 정확히는 relativity의 metric signature 때문에 pseudo-Riemannian geometry를 씁니다.

Manifold

Spacetime as a 4D manifold

Spacetime은 locally flat하게 보입니다. 작은 영역에서는 ℝ4처럼 다룰 수 있지만, globally는 curved structure를 가질 수 있습니다. 각 점에는 velocity와 momentum이 사는 tangent space가 있습니다.

gμν

Metric tensor

Metric tensor는 spacetime의 geometry를 encode합니다. 거리, 각도, volume, relativity에서는 proper time과 causal structure까지 정합니다.

Γρμν

Christoffel symbols

Christoffel symbols는 connection입니다. 곡면 위를 움직일 때 vector를 어떻게 미분하고 parallel transport할지 알려주며, metric과 그 derivatives에서 계산됩니다.

Rρσμν

Riemann curvature tensor

Riemann tensor는 intrinsic curvature를 측정합니다. Vector를 closed loop를 따라 옮겼을 때 돌아와서 방향이 달라진다면, 그 차이가 curvature 정보를 담습니다.

3. Building the Einstein Tensor

슬라이드의 핵심 흐름은 metric에서 시작해 curvature 정보를 점점 압축하고, 마지막에 conservation과 맞는 Einstein tensor를 만드는 것입니다.

1

Metric tensor

gμν가 every point에서 distance와 angle을 정합니다. Geometry의 시작점입니다.

2

Christoffel symbols

Basis vector가 manifold 위에서 어떻게 바뀌는지 정의합니다. 즉, covariant derivative의 재료입니다.

3

Riemann curvature tensor

Spacetime의 full intrinsic curvature를 담습니다.

4

Ricci tensor

Rμν = Rρμρν. Riemann tensor를 contraction해서 volume이 curvature 아래에서 어떻게 바뀌는지 남깁니다.

5

Ricci scalar

R = gμνRμν. 각 점에서 total scalar curvature를 주는 하나의 수입니다.

6

Einstein tensor

Gμν = Rμν - 1/2 Rgμν. Rμν와 R의 divergence-free 조합입니다.

기억할 순서: metric → connection → Riemann curvature → Ricci tensor → Ricci scalar → Einstein tensor. EFE는 이 마지막 curvature package를 stress-energy tensor와 연결합니다.

4. Energy-Momentum Tensor

Tμν는 Einstein gravity에서 gravitational field의 source입니다. 단순히 mass만이 아니라 energy, momentum flow, pressure, shear까지 포함합니다.

Component Physical interpretation Plain reading
T00 Energy density, time-time component 단위 부피당 energy가 얼마나 있는가.
T0i Energy flux, time-space component 단위 면적을 통해 energy가 얼마나 흐르는가.
Ti0 Momentum density, space-time component 단위 부피당 momentum이 얼마나 있는가.
Tij Stress, space-space component Pressure와 shear force가 단위 면적에 어떻게 작용하는가.
Physics requires energy-momentum conservation. 그래서 source side는 ∇μTμν = 0을 만족해야 하고, 이것이 다음 Bianchi identity와 맞물립니다.

5. Bianchi Identity

여기서는 contraction 계산을 길게 쓰지 않고, Bianchi identity가 무엇인지와 왜 중요한지만 정리합니다.

What

Curvature의 기하학적 제약

Bianchi identity는 curvature가 임의로 변할 수 없다고 말합니다. 슬라이드 표현대로, covariant exterior derivative of curvature vanishes identically입니다.

Contracted

Einstein tensor의 divergence가 0

적절히 contraction하면 ∇μGμν = 0이 됩니다. 그래서 Einstein tensor는 자동으로 divergence-free인 curvature 조합입니다.

Why

EFE가 conservation과 맞아야 함

EFE에서 Gμν가 Tμν와 같아지려면, geometry side의 conservation 구조가 matter side의 ∇μTμν = 0과 맞아야 합니다.

6. Solving the EFE

EFE는 4×4 symmetric rank-2 tensor라서 10개의 independent equation을 갖는 nonlinear PDE system입니다. 일반적인 analytic solution은 사실상 불가능합니다.

Symmetry

Analytic solutions

강한 symmetry assumption과 vacuum condition Tμν = 0을 두면 식이 크게 단순해집니다. Minkowski, Schwarzschild, Kerr metric이 대표적인 예입니다.

Numerical

Numerical relativity

Binary black holes나 neutron-star mergers처럼 실제적인 system은 symmetry가 거의 없습니다. 이때는 EFE를 numerical integration해서 gravitational-wave waveform, ray tracing, black-hole 주변 field를 계산합니다.

Metric Assumption Description
Minkowski Flat spacetime, no matter Special relativity의 stage.
Schwarzschild Spherical, non-rotating, vacuum Static black hole geometry.
Kerr Axisymmetric, rotating, vacuum Rotating black hole geometry.
